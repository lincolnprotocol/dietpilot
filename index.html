How We Calculate Your Needs

Your basal metabolic rate (BMR) and total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) are calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation—a scientifically validated formula.

Men: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) - (5 × age in yrs) + 5
Women: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) - (5 × age in yrs) - 161

We multiply your BMR by your activity factor to find your TDEE, ensuring safe, personalized caloric targets.
